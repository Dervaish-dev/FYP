## Test Voice Journal 🎙️

1. Go to Journal tab
2. Tap **"Voice Journal"** button (blue with mic icon)
3. Grant microphone permission
4. WebView dialog opens with Retell AI
5. **Toggle Speaker** (🔊 on/off button)
6. Speak naturally about your day (15-30 seconds)
7. Tap **"End Call & Save"**
8. Wait 5-10 seconds for processing
9. New journal entry appears! ✅
