🔍 KEY OBSERVATIONS:

1. HuggingFace returns numbers like 0.8723456789
   → We round/format to 87.2% for display

2. All 7 emotions always returned (scores add to ~1.0)
   → We show top 5 to user to avoid clutter

3. "fear" → "Worried" in our app
   → We rename to be more user-friendly

4. Intensity auto-calculated from confidence
   → 0.87 × 10 = 8.7 → rounds to 9

5. High confidence (>50%) = auto-saved
   → Entry goes straight to emotion history

🧪 TO TEST IT YOURSELF:

1. Install the APK on your phone
2. Open NeuroCompanion app
3. Go to "Emotions" screen
4. Tap camera icon
5. Select a selfie with clear facial expression
6. Tap "Analyze with AI"
7. Watch the logs (connect via USB debugging):
   adb logcat | grep -E "flutter|Facial"

You'll see exactly what I showed above! ✅
